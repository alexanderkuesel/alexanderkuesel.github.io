About

I’m an electromechanical engineer who ended up spending his career translating between two worlds that rarely speak the same language: the plant floor and the software team. For a decade that meant distributed control systems — Ovation controllers, OPC, fault-tolerant design, commissioning power plants across North and South America. More recently it has meant leading engineers who wire robots, PLCs and vision models into the same data pipeline.

Most of my work has been about making industrial data useful: getting it out of proprietary systems, into places where it can be modelled, and back into control loops where it changes what the plant actually does. Lately that includes putting LLMs to work on manufacturing documentation and telemetry without pretending they’re a control system.

I’m currently finishing a master’s in mechanical engineering focused on energy management at the Universidad de Costa Rica. Outside of work you’ll usually find me on a trail — teaching wilderness skills, mapping wildlife corridors, or chasing a signal on a handheld radio.
